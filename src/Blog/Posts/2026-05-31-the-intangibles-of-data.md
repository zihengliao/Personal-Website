## The Intangibles of Data


I love basketball. Specifically, I love the spurs. They play a team game, they are all committed to winning. I don't think the data is able to show the connection they have as a team.

This article is written 30 minutes after the Spurs beat OKC in game 7 and as a fan, the emotions are still very high.

There is one specific play that I want to highlight which the stats do not even come close to representing. 

![Box Score](/assets/blog/2026-05-31-the-intangibles-of-data/box_score.jpeg)

Looking at the stat sheet, you would probably assume Luke Kornet was playing garbage minutes towards the end of the game where his impact wasn't very significant.

Even if he wasn't playing in garbage time, it doesn't seem like he contributed much. 

0-3 in FG, 4 rebounds and a +1 plus-minus score with 6 minutes of playing time just doesn't scream impact on the overall game.

I'd beg to differ. 

Take a look at the following [clip](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=uXFVN4XynCs) of Luke Kornet's block on OKC's Hartenstein. I'd invite you to watch the entire 2 minutes to really understand the point I'm trying to make.

The immediate impact is obvious, Kornet, gets a block, Castle converts it to 2 points.

What isn't measured is the morale boost it gave to the team. 

Take a look at the reactions of the Spurs bench. Everybody watching this live intuitively understood the impact of this one play.

It's a momentum shift. After this one play, the Spurs went to score 7 consecutive points forcing the Thunder to take their crucial last time-out with 4 and a half minutes left to go.

This would eventually lead to the Thunder unable to call a time out with 1 and a half minutes left on the clock in a 2 possession game resulting in a Spurs win.

The butterfly effect of this one play is insane.

The more I continue to watch the game of basketball, the more I realise the box-score often times do the players injustice.

Here are some clips of people talking about the impact of that one play.

[Wemby on Kornet's block](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Gjb0knIPj0s&t=82s)

[Spurs coach Mitch Johnson on Kornet's block](https://youtu.be/usFMbW5mDsU?si=9k_USTKIUNBmy7Jy&t=260)

There will most likely be analysts talking about this play in the near future as well, but looking at the comments discussing this, everybody just knew intuitively the impact it had.

It's almost like the morale of an entire army just shifting. You can just feel it but words can't describe it.
